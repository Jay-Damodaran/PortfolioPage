Participated in the 6th Annual Energy AI Hackathon hosted by UT Austin’s Hildebrand Department of Petroleum and Geosystems Engineering. Competed with 45 other student teams comprised of students from across the university over a continuous 48 hours. Mentored by UT PGE faculty, graduate student architects, as well as industry professionals from Chevron, Coterra Energy, ExxonMobil, Neuralix, BP, ConocoPhillips, and Elk Range Royalties.
- Developed a machine learning workflow in Python to predict cumulative three-year oil production with uncertainty for preproduction vertical wells using well-log measurements, 2D seismic-derived facies maps, and production histories from 71 existing wells
- Performed data visualization and preliminary statistical analysis
- Utilized MICE algorithm for missing value imputation
- Performed step-wise regression and SHAP analysis for feature selection
- Applied feature engineering, model selection and tuning, including automatic hyperparameter optimization using optuna library, and uncertainty quantification to generate both point estimates and 100 probabilistic realizations for 12 new wells
- Utilized Shapley plots for model explainability and feature importance quantification
