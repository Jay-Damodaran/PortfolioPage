Leading a team of engineers to develop an inexpensive, low-power, intelligent EMG armband with on-device signal processing and gesture recognition as well as BLE angle of arrival capabilities for additional degrees of control. This device allows users to control hardware, such as a rover and a prosthetic hand, through hand and arm gestures. Current medical grade EMGs cost $1000s, so a chief objective of this project is to test the limits of edge compute and edge AI while balancing performance, latency, energy consumption, and financial cost considerations. 

**Novelties/State of the Art Project Goals**
- edge DSP/AI
- BLE Angle of Arrival
- cost
