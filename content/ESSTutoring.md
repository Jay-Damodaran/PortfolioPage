Worked three nights a week as a tutor in the Engineering Education and Research Center (EER), where I tutored Engineering students in Calculus, Differential Equations, and Physics Mechanics.
- Held open drop-in hours across several of the highest-attrition gateway courses in the Cockrell curriculum, giving students a consistent place to receive help outside of class
- Worked with students individually through problem sets, guiding them toward the solution rather than providing it directly, so that they developed the reasoning needed to approach similar problems independently
- Maintained sufficient breadth across the curriculum to address the range of coursework students brought to a given session
- Reinforced core mathematical and physical intuition that students carry forward into upper-division engineering coursework
