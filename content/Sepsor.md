SEPSOR was my 3rd year biomedical design semester long group project. I worked in a team of biomedical engineering students to develop a biomedical device while adhering to FDA design control (now called Design & Development) standards. We developed a Sepsis early warning prototype system that tracks certain physiological indicators of Sepsis like Heart Rate and Respiratory Rate using an HR/pulse ox module as well as an accelerometer placed on the chest. When metrics crossed certain literature-backed risk thresholds, we activated a buzzer that would notify the user to seek medical attention. 

On the documentation/project management side, we had to rigorously adhere to FDA design control standards per ISO 13485 and 21 CFR 820. We had to identify user needs and conduct market research and submit a document for this. We also had to develop a project management plan, write a Requirement Specifications Document (Design Input Document), maintain a Requirements Traceability Matrix, write a Design Specification Document (Design Ouput Document), and produce a final Design History File (Design and Development File). Our documents also had to undergo a Requirements Review and Design Review from our peers with version-tracked modifications made at each stage.
