I was part of a team of 5 students representing Longhorn Neurotech in the Global NeuroHack 2026 competition in April, 2026, which was a neurotechnology competition featuring teams from universities around the world such as Oxford, McGill, EPFL, and NUS. 

Our team competed under e184's speech decoding track and placed 2nd with our project FABLE, an adaptive semantic BCI storybook that helps users, especially those with aphasia, engage with language and develop semantic connections. We outplaced the teams from UC Berkely and Colombia University.

Over the course of two days, we spent hours whiteboarding, collecting data, building the proper EEG feature extraction pipeline, training ML models, and setting up a UI for a demo. I'm excited to see where we take FABLE in the future!
