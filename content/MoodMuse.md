Participated in the RecordHacks Hackathon along with 3 other teammates to produce a project that is music themed 

- Collaborated on Mood Muse, an emotionally aware music player that uses a live video feed along with a mood detection model to identify the user's mood and play music accordingly
- Designed a supplemental image classification CNN to address biases and edge cases in pretrained model 
- Implemented post-processing and smoothing on mood predictions, preventing excessive music change during live inference
